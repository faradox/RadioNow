Daily Morning Broadcast

Every day at the set time (Europe/Berlin) the app pulls world headlines from public RSS feeds and has the day's host write a comforting, satirical take on them. Hosts rotate by date. At most one run per day; failed runs retry up to 3 times, 15 minutes apart, within 6 hours of the scheduled time.

The lens every morning script is written through. Clear the box and save to go back to the built-in default.
